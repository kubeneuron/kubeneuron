## How to sign

Before your first pull request can be merged, add this comment to the pull
request:

> I have read the KubeNeuron Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0, and
> I agree to it.

Post it exactly as shown, as a comment of its own. The CLA check runs again
when a maintainer re-runs it or when you push a new commit; it then records
your GitHub account, the date, and the pull request in this repository and
turns green.

That comment, linked to your GitHub account, records your agreement. Your
agreement covers all your later Contributions until a new version of this
Agreement is published and you are asked to agree to it.
